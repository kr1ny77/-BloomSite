# Bloom website replacement

Mode: Persuade. Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/bloom-new.png`.
User approved the comp and delegated subsequent implementation decisions.

## Direction

Editorial laboratory: natural food photography, near-black forest canvas,
warm off-white reading surfaces, fresh lime as one accent. Original Bloom
identity stays. One finished information postcard introduces the separate app.

## First viewport

Slim navigation, two-line Russian headline at left, still-life photograph
at right, two disabled store placeholders. Real HTML text overlays a separately
produced photograph. Below it a light editorial introduction begins.

## Story and motion

Composition and nutrition, an explained illustrative score, source provenance,
three static iPhone-style app previews, final invitation. Timed scene entry
holds forward scrolling briefly until the visible animation finishes. Backward
navigation and Escape release it. Reduced motion gives unrestricted reading.
Long sections remain readable. Text remains visible throughout transitions.

## Fidelity and constraints

Preserve the approved comp's topology and palette. Use the original logo in
place of its generated botanical approximation. Use readable text contrast and
responsive type. No app functions, forms, collection, external store links or
invented research. Native Go server and static Sites build remain supported.
