## menu-ingredients

Source: assets/generated/menu-ingredients.png

No packaging, no yogurt, no phones, no hands, no text, no interfaces, no collage.

## menu-barcode

Source: assets/generated/menu-barcode.png
Runtime: static/images/menu-barcode.webp

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: square visual for Bloom website menu, represents finding a product by barcode. Subject: a beautifully crafted small translucent pale jade glass block with a cream paper label wrapped over its gently rounded edge, vertical dark green barcode stripes printed on the label, one narrow warm lime scanning light crossing the stripes. Tactile paper, optical glass refraction, photographic studio details, understated editorial product photography on solid deep forest green. Single object fills middle 60 percent of square. No numbers or text, no phone, no yogurt, no hand, no UI, no floating particles, no neon sci-fi glow.

Source: assets/yogurt-model/botanical-print.png

Wide horizontal 3:1 composition of lush expressive painted botanical leaves and wild meadow flowers at the lower edge, extending into both sides, with a generous empty warm ivory center and upper area for typography added later. Verdant green leaves with coral poppies, tiny blue cornflowers, creamy daisies and lime leaf accents. Premium contemporary European food packaging art, crisp gouache, rich color, detailed organic stems and petals. No words, no letters, no typography, no container, no mockup, no shadow, no border.
